# How to Get Support
This page provides instructions on how to get support with Microsoft Collaborate.

## Support Options
1. If you are not sure how to do something in Microsoft Collaborate, review the [Documentation and Guidance](/collaborate/) for the area you have questions about.
2. If your question or issue is related to a specific program or engagement, check their support options first. This information is available on the program and engagement overview pages respectively.
3. If something is not working the way you expect, check the [Troubleshooting Guides](/collaborate/troubleshooting) to see if your issue is described. 

## Customer Support
  * Navigate to https://aka.ms/dcsupport.
  * **Sign in**.
  * Click **Contact Us**.
  * Go to **Non-Technical Support - Dashboard** section.

>[!NOTE]
>
> You must sign in to chat or to submit an incident.<br>

## Submit an Incident
Please read the below items before clicking on [Submit an Incident](https://support.serviceshub.microsoft.com/supportforbusiness/create?sapId=bc9d4067-7218-61b9-1d2c-68ae591acf9d) to prevent any delay.

* Provide clear repro steps of the issue along with any error messages. 
* Make sure you create the support request under the user account with this issue.
* Create multiple cases may cause slower responses. 
* Please check your junk folder in case the mail server classifies Partner Center Support’s reply as junk.

There are two ways to open a support case: 

> 1. Open a case in Partner Cetner (recommended for Collaborate Account Settings or Enrollment questions)

Partner Center now support topics that route directly to the Collaborate Program support team.

  1. Go to [Partner Center](https://partner.microsoft.com/dashboard/home).
  2. Sign in with your Partner Center account that is enrolled in the Collaborate Program.
  3. Select AI Assistant and Support from the top right.
  4. In the How can I help? Select Account Settings Topic.
  5. Type “Collaborate enrollment” or “Collaborate profile”.
  6. Click Contact Support.
  7. Workspace
     a. If this is an Account Settings question it’s already populated.
     b. If this is an Enrollment question, change the workspace to Enrollment. 
 8. In Problem Type enter “Collaborate“.
    a.  This will display all available Collaborate topics for either Account Settings or Enrollment under All Enrollment or Account Settings Topics.
 9. Select the most appropriate topic for your question.
 10. Click Review Solutions. 
    a.  These will constantly be updated with common self-serve scenarios.
 11. If that does not solve your problem Click Create support request.
 12. Add as many details in the Problem Details section, Upload files with any screenshots to help support understand the issue.
 13. Complete Contact Info.
 14. Review.
 15. Click Submit request.

>[!NOTE]
>
> We are working to expand the Partner AI Assistant to include Collaborate Learn documentation.


 > 2. Open a case in Microsoft Engage Center (required for Program and engagement management support).
    
 Microsoft Engage Center is the fastest path to reach the Collaborate Program support team for Program and engagement management. 

  1. Go to the Collaborate Program support creation form in Microsoft Engage Center.
  2. Sign in with your Partner Center account that is enrolled in the Collaborate Program. 

>[!NOTE]
>
> You must sign in with an account that is enrolled in the Collaborate Program. If you sign in with a different account, you are blocked from selecting the correct routing choices during the creation workflow. If you can't sign in with an enrolled account, use the following steps instead.<br> 

  3. When creating the case, select the following values: 

|Field        | Value                                                                      |
|-------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|Product      | Collaborate                                                                |
|Problem Type | \<Select the appropriate topic> Options: Program and Engagement Management, Account Settings or Enrollment |

  4. Complete the form and submit the case. A confirmation email with a support request number will be sent to the contacts confirming receipt of the submission.
